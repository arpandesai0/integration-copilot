# Provider icons

These icons identify the provider products; they do not imply endorsement.

- `jira.ico`: downloaded from the official Jira site, https://jira.atlassian.com/favicon.ico
- `xbox.ico`: downloaded from the official Xbox site, https://www.xbox.com/favicon.ico

Retrieved 2026-10-29. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.
Discord, LinkedIn, Spotify, GitHub, Slack, and Google Drive use inline brand marks in the provider icon component and therefore do not depend on remote favicon availability. Other researched providers request `/favicon.ico` from their resolved official domain and fall back to the provider initial when unavailable.
